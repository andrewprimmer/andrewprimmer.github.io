Microteach 9.1 · 1:10Hire to retire in 2 minutes

The HR and payroll cycle covers the whole employee journey: recruiting and hiring, onboarding, tracking time and attendance, running payroll, managing benefits, and…

Read the script

For many companies, people are the biggest cost. Hire to retire tracks every dollar.

The HR and payroll cycle covers the whole employee journey: recruiting and hiring, onboarding, tracking time and attendance, running payroll, managing benefits, and finally the employee's exit.

It has two halves. HR maintains the employee master data: hiring, job, pay rate and deductions. Payroll uses that data, plus time worked, to pay people.

Key documents include the employee master record, time cards, the payroll register, paychecks or direct deposits, and tax forms.

The key control: the people who hire staff and set pay rates should be separate from the people who process payroll.

So: hire, track, pay, and exit, with HR and payroll kept apart.

Quick check. Who should maintain employee pay rates: HR or payroll? Pause and pick.

HR. Payroll processes pay, but shouldn't be able to change pay rates.

Microteach 9.2 · 1:21HCM systems

Human capital management, or HCM, software manages the whole workforce: HR records, recruiting, performance, learning, time, payroll and benefits.

Read the script

Payroll used to be a box of time cards. Now it's a cloud system called HCM.

Human capital management, or HCM, software manages the whole workforce: HR records, recruiting, performance, learning, time, payroll and benefits.

Major systems include Workday HCM, SAP SuccessFactors, Oracle Cloud HCM, ADP Workforce Now, and UKG.

Payroll can run inside the HCM, or be outsourced to a provider like ADP or Paychex. Either way, the payroll journal must post to the ERP's general ledger.

Modern HCMs offer employee self-service: staff update their own addresses, bank details and tax elections. That saves time, but needs strong authentication.

HCMs hold very sensitive data: Social Security numbers, bank accounts, salaries. Access must be tightly restricted.

So HCM is one system for the whole workforce, feeding payroll and the ledger.

Quick check. Which of these is an HCM system? Pause and pick.

SAP SuccessFactors.

Microteach 9.3 · 1:10Processing a payroll

The seven steps: update payroll master data, validate time and attendance data, prepare the payroll, disburse pay, calculate employer-paid benefits and taxes, pay…

Read the script

Running payroll is a seven-step process. Miss a step, and people don't get paid.

The seven steps: update payroll master data, validate time and attendance data, prepare the payroll, disburse pay, calculate employer-paid benefits and taxes, pay over the taxes and deductions, and post the journal entry to the general ledger.

Payroll goes from gross to net. Gross pay is hours times rate, or salary. Subtract taxes, retirement contributions and other deductions, and you get net pay.

The payroll register lists every employee's gross pay, deductions and net pay. It's reviewed before payment is released.

Many companies use a separate payroll bank account, funded with exactly the net pay total. When all the paychecks clear, it returns to zero.

So: validate the data, calculate gross to net, review the register, then pay.

Quick check. Gross pay minus deductions equals what? Pause and pick.

Net pay.

Microteach 9.4 · 1:23US payroll taxes and forms

From the employee's pay, the employer withholds federal income tax, state and local income tax where they apply, Social Security at six point two percent, and…

Read the script

A US paycheck has more deductions than you might expect. Here are the main ones.

From the employee's pay, the employer withholds federal income tax, state and local income tax where they apply, Social Security at six point two percent, and Medicare at one point four five percent.

Social Security and Medicare together are called FICA taxes, after the Federal Insurance Contributions Act. The employer pays a matching amount.

Employers also pay their matching FICA, plus unemployment taxes: FUTA at the federal level and SUTA at the state level.

Key forms: the W-4, where employees set their withholding; Form nine forty-one, the quarterly federal payroll return; the W-2, each employee's annual wage statement; and Form nine forty, the annual FUTA return.

Withheld taxes are trust fund taxes: the money belongs to the government. Failing to pay them over is one of the most serious payroll mistakes a company can make.

So: withhold, match, file and pay, all on time.

Quick check. Which form reports an employee's annual wages and withholding? Pause and pick.

The W-2.

Microteach 9.5 · 1:07Ghost employees and payroll fraud

A ghost employee is someone on the payroll who doesn't actually work there: a made-up person, or a former employee who was never removed.

Read the script

Some payroll checks go to people who don't exist. They're called ghost employees.

A ghost employee is someone on the payroll who doesn't actually work there: a made-up person, or a former employee who was never removed.

Other schemes include inflated hours, unauthorized pay rate increases, fake overtime, and buddy punching, where a friend clocks in for you.

Controls: separate HR, timekeeping and payroll duties; review new hires and terminations; use biometric time clocks; and compare payroll records to HR's headcount.

Analytics can flag ghosts: duplicate bank accounts, employees with no tax withheld, pay after a termination date, and addresses shared with other staff.

The goal is simple: every paycheck goes to a real person, for real work.

Quick check. A former employee who's still being paid is an example of what? Pause and pick.

A ghost employee.

Microteach 9.6 · 1:13Outsourcing payroll

A payroll service bureau, such as ADP or Paychex, processes payroll for you: calculating pay, making deposits, and filing payroll taxes.

Read the script

Many companies don't run payroll themselves. They hand it to a specialist.

A payroll service bureau, such as ADP or Paychex, processes payroll for you: calculating pay, making deposits, and filing payroll taxes.

Benefits: lower cost, specialist tax expertise, fewer filing errors and penalties, and staff freed up for other work.

Risks: less direct control, sensitive data shared with a third party, and dependence on that provider.

So how does your auditor trust someone else's system? Through a SOC one report: an independent auditor's report on the provider's controls that affect financial reporting.

Outsourcing doesn't outsource responsibility. You still review the payroll reports, control the data you send, and perform the user controls the SOC report says you need.

So: you can outsource the work, but not the responsibility.

Quick check. Which report gives assurance over a payroll provider's controls? Pause and pick.

A SOC one report.
