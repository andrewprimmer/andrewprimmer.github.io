Teaching · Distance learning

ACCT 360 Accounting Information Systems

A complete mock course for distance learning: 14 lectures, each taught in six two-minute videos and finished with an interactive exercise set. It covers ERP systems, the five transaction cycles, internal control and audit.

This course explains how accounting information systems work and how to evaluate them. It covers the five principal transaction cycles: revenue, expenditure, production, human resources and payroll, and the general ledger. It looks closely at the ERP systems most used by US companies, including SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics, Workday and NetSuite, and then turns to how these systems are controlled and audited.

How to study it

  1. Work through the lectures in order. Each has six microteaches of under two minutes each, made to be watched on a phone.
  2. When a video asks a quick-check question, pause it and answer before the reveal.
  3. Finish each lecture with its exercise set. Wrong answers point you back to the video to rewatch.
  4. Use the scripts under each video for revision, or if you'd rather read than watch.
ACCT 360 is a mock course: a model of how a full module can be taught at a distance in short videos. The 84 videos run to about 1.8 hours in total.

Lectures

Part one · Foundations

  1. L1Intro to AISWhat an AIS is, its six components, the value chain, the five cycles, its users, and why accountants need systems skills.
  2. L2Documenting processesData flow diagrams, flowcharts and BPMN, source and turnaround documents, and the common mistakes to avoid.
  3. L3Data and databasesFiles versus databases, keys and relationships, the REA model, normalization, SQL and data warehouses.

Part two · ERP systems

  1. L4ERP fundamentalsWhat ERP is, how it evolved, modules and cycles, master data, deployment options, and why projects fail.
  2. L5The ERP landscapeSAP S/4HANA, Oracle Fusion and NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Workday, mid-market systems, and how firms choose.

Part three · The transaction cycles

  1. L6Revenue cycleOrder to cash: sales orders and credit, shipping, billing and ASC 606, collections, and the cycle's controls.
  2. L7Expenditure cycleProcure to pay: requisitions and POs, receiving, the three-way match, disbursements, and expenditure fraud.
  3. L8Production cyclePlan to produce: BOMs and routings, push versus pull, costing systems, inventory controls and IoT data.
  4. L9HR and payroll cycleHire to retire: HCM systems, processing a payroll, US payroll taxes and forms, payroll fraud and outsourcing.
  5. L10General ledger and reportingRecord to report: the chart of accounts, journal entries, the month-end close, XBRL, and dashboards.

Part four · Control and audit

  1. L11Fraud and internal controlThe fraud triangle, SOX 302 and 404, COSO and COSO ERM, control types, and segregation of duties in an ERP.
  2. L12IT controls and cybersecurityGeneral and application controls, COBIT, access controls, encryption, processing integrity and disaster recovery.
  3. L13Auditing AISTypes of audit, the risk-based approach, auditing around and through the computer, CAATs, and SOC reports.
  4. L14Emerging technologiesRPA, machine learning, generative AI, blockchain, process mining, and a two-minute recap of the course.
