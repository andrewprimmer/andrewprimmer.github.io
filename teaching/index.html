Teaching

Courses and teaching materials in financial accounting, accounting information systems and management accounting. Everything here is free to use, and most of it is built to be studied on a phone, in two-minute videos with interactive exercises.

Distance-learning courses

  • ACCT 211 Principles of Accounting I Introductory financial accounting in 14 lectures and 84 two-minute videos: the accounting cycle, merchandising, internal control and cash, receivables, inventory, long-term assets and current liabilities. Every video is tied to sections and pages of the free OpenStax textbook, with 109 interactive exercises and the Redwood River Books bookkeeping simulator. Textbook: OpenStax, Principles of Accounting, Volume 1 · 14 lectures · slides · simulator
  • ACCT 360 Accounting Information Systems ERP systems, the five transaction cycles, internal control and audit, in 14 lectures and 84 two-minute videos, with lecture slides, exercise sets a working ERP simulator with six guided missions, and Rails & Ledgers, a railway accounting game set in 1896–1930. 14 lectures · slides · ERP simulator · railway game
  • ACCT 414 Federal Tax I Federal income taxation of individuals in 14 lectures and 84 two-minute videos, on 2026 law including the One Big Beautiful Bill Act: the tax system and the IRS, planning, the tax formula, income and exclusions, deductions, investments, credits, business income, depreciation, property sales, compensation, retirement and home ownership. One Marshall, Minnesota family's return is built up through the course, with 112 interactive exercises. Textbook: McGraw Hill's Taxation of Individuals, 2027 release · 14 lectures · slides

Interactive course

  • Foundations Accounting from the Archive Seven interactive lessons in the foundations of accounting, from the accounting equation and double entry to disclosure and tax, each worked through a company from the history of British enterprise overseas. No prior accounting knowledge is assumed. For MSc Accounting and Finance students starting from scratch

Short video lessons

  • Intermediate Revenue Recognition in Six Videos Two-minute lessons on the ASC 606 five-step model, made for revising on your phone after class, with interactive journal-entry practice.
  • Intermediate Accounts Receivable: Bad Debts The direct write-off and allowance methods, worked from one aged debtor report for a Marshall, Minnesota distributor, with year-end journal entries to practise.

Accounting with AI

  • Management Management Accounting with Claude for Excel A year of data for Halden Motors, a fictional UK car maker, with exercises for Claude for Excel and Google Gemini on variances, costing, overheads, financial statement analysis and aged debtors, each with a worked solution.
  • Workshop AI and Automation in Accounting A three-hour practical session. Use AI to write a ratio analyser and an aged debtor review in Google Colab, then find the errors planted in the data. Datasets, copy-ready prompts, slides and a phone-friendly exit quiz. No coding experience needed · Google Colab · Gemini or Claude
