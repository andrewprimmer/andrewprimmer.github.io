Teaching · ACCT 211 Principles of Accounting I · Simulator

Redwood River Books

Keep the books of a small Minnesota business for its first year. Every document in the in-tray becomes a journal entry, and every entry you post updates the ledger, the trial balance and the financial statements.

How it works

  1. Choose a mission. Each one matches a part of the course, and you can start anywhere: earlier missions are brought forward as opening balances.
  2. Read the document on the left. Work out which accounts it changes, whether each goes up or down, and by how much.
  3. Enter the journal entry and press Post entry. If it's wrong, the feedback tells you whether the problem is an account, a side or an amount. After two tries you can see the answer.
  4. Watch the trial balance, T-accounts and statements below change with every entry.
